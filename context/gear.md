This is the gear I use to make my photos. I started out with a DSLR and have since moved to mirrorless — but I still keep the old workhorse around.

## Cameras

- **Nikon Z6 II** — primary, full-frame mirrorless
- **Nikon D700** — secondary, full-frame DSLR (where it all started)

## Lenses

- **AF-S NIKKOR 16–35mm f/4G ED VR** — wide-angle zoom, F-mount
- **AF-S NIKKOR 24–120mm f/4G ED VR** — all-round zoom, F-mount
- **AF-S NIKKOR 70–200mm f/4G ED VR** — telephoto zoom, F-mount
- **Meike 85mm f/1.8 STM** — portrait prime, Z-mount

## Adapters

- **Nikon FTZ II** — F-mount to Z-mount adapter, full AF and electronic aperture support

## Accessories

- **FAIRCO Focus F-1** — quick-release shoulder sling strap
- **Decathlon Explorer 900 30L** — hiking backpack, used as camera bag on day trips
